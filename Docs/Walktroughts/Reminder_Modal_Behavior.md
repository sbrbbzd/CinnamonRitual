# Reminder Modal Behavior Walkthrough

## Overview
This document explains the updated behavior of the Reminder Settings modal.

## User Experience
When the "Set Reminder" modal appears (e.g., after completing a ritual or accessing settings):

1.  **Interaction**:
    - You can toggle Morning, Afternoon, and Evening reminders.
    - The "SAVE" button is disabled until at least one option is selected.

2.  **Closing the Modal**:
    - **Previously**: You could close the modal by tapping outside or pressing the back button.
    - **Now**: You MUST click the **SAVE** button to confirm your choices and close the modal.
    - Tapping the background does nothing.
    - Pressing the back button does nothing.

## Rationale
This ensures that you deliberately choose your reminder settings without accidentally losing your context or dismissing the prompt before making a decision.
